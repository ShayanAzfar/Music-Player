# Music Player
## Offline Music Player so you can listen to your favorite music offline.

## Description:
### Its an offline music player in which you can listen to songs offline, which is made from simple html css and javascript and also that has dark theme and light theme which you can change by only a switching theme button. i have also added raining effect with the help of keyframs of css.

## Why i made this?
### To listen to my spotify playlist songs offline.

## How to listen the song yourself?
### I have added control buttons by which you can pause and play the songs and move to next or previous song by clicking on controls icons. You can also change between light dark mode themes by clicking the moon or sun icon at your top left of the screen.

## Ai Usage?
### No Ai is used in the overall code, just for finding bugs and manually fixing it.

## Inspiration?
### To listen spotify playlist without paying for subscription or downloading the songs.
